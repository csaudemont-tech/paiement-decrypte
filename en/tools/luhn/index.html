What the key does not say

Luhn is a typing check. It catches every single digit error and almost every swap of two neighbouring digits, except 09 into 90. A correct key says nothing about whether the card exists, whether it is active, or who issued it: the test numbers above belong to nobody.

Do not paste a real number here. Nothing leaves this page, but a card number has no business sitting in a clipboard, or in a browser history.

The same check runs everywhere, from the terminal keypad to the issuer. The rest is told in the episodes.
