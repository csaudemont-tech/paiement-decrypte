Who writes

In payments, everyone knows their own link well, few people see the whole chain. Paiement Décrypté takes one mechanism at a time and explains it in five minutes, quizzes included.

I’m Charles Saudemont. I’ve worked in payments for ten years and had the chance to work on several links of that chain: terminals and their after-sales, card issuing, and today the architecture of an in-store payment solution, from the terminal to acquiring. I care about sharing my experience and expertise to make payments accessible and let as many people as possible understand this world.
